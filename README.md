Unholy Death Knight Target Helper (This addon was made by IA)

A lightweight addon designed specifically for Unholy Death Knights to help you maximize your damage output!

What it does:
The addon automatically displays the optimal spell to cast based on the number of enemies around you. It shows a clear icon on your screen that switches between Death Coil (for 1-3 targets) and Epidemic (for 4+ targets), taking the guesswork out of your rotation.

Smart detection:

Automatically appears when you enter combat
Counts nearby enemies based on threat and combat status
Integrates with Army of the Dead buff, suggesting upgraded abilities when active (Necrotic Coil and Graveyard)
Shows enemy count and spell name for quick reference
Fully customizable:

Drag and position the icon anywhere on your screen
Adjust icon size (32-128 pixels)
Control transparency (10-100%)
Toggle text display on/off
All settings are saved automatically
Easy to use:
Type /aoedk to open the options panel, or use /aoedk unlock to move the icon around. That's it! The addon does the rest, letting you focus on crushing your enemies.
